Dear Mr Georgiou,

thank you very much for your reply and for sending me the announcement of the Scholarships of Academic Excellence for Doctoral Studies for the Fall Semester 2026.

Having read it, I now understand that each scholarship is tied to a specific thematic area proposed and supervised by a named member of the faculty, and that the Fall 2026 round covered Medical Sciences, Exercise Health and Nutrition, Physiotherapy and Law. That explains why no scholarship was available in Business Administration, and I am grateful for the clarification. I am equally grateful for the confirmation of the 10% reduction on tuition fees as a continuing EUC student.

As I am applying for the **February 2027 (Spring) intake**, may I ask five short questions:

1. Is this scholarship scheme announced **only for Fall intakes**, or is a corresponding round also held for the Spring semester? Every announcement I have been able to find refers to a Fall semester.

2. If the scheme is indeed Fall-only: could a student **commencing doctoral studies in February 2027** be considered in the Fall 2027 round, or does the condition "exclusively for new doctoral students" rule that out?

3. How does a thematic area come to be included in the list? If it is **proposed by the supervising faculty member**, by what date would a proposal need to be submitted for the next round? My prospective supervisor, Prof. Alexios Kythreotis, would then be able to consider this in good time.

4. Is the **10% alumni reduction** applied automatically upon admission or does it require a separate request? Does it apply for the full duration of the programme, and could it be combined with a scholarship should one become available later?

5. For my financial planning, could you confirm the **tuition fee and payment schedule** for the PhD in Business Administration — the amount payable per semester or per year, and any registration or examination fees payable in addition to tuition?

The reason I ask in this detail is that I am also applying to German scholarship foundations for a doctoral grant. They require a precise statement of the tuition costs and of any support received from the university.

Thank you again for your time and assistance.
