Dear Mr Kounoupis,

thank you again for forwarding my earlier enquiry to Mr Georgiou, who has been very helpful regarding the doctoral scholarships and the tuition fee structure.

I am writing to you now about a different matter, which I believe falls within your area: **financial aid based on personal circumstances**, as distinct from merit scholarships.

My situation briefly: I am completing my MSc at European University Cyprus in January 2027 and am applying for the PhD in Business Administration for the February 2027 intake in Nicosia. I **finance my studies entirely from my own earnings**; my family is not in a position to contribute, and I am the first person in my family to attend university. I am currently working and saving in order to fund the move to Cyprus.

May I ask:

1. Does the University offer **need-based financial aid** for doctoral students, and if so, what are the criteria and how does one apply?

2. Mr Georgiou mentioned that the tuition instalments can be extended to **up to twelve** through my Academic Advisor. Is that arrangement also available to doctoral students from the start of the programme, and whom should I approach to request it?

3. Are there any **hardship funds, emergency support, or deferred payment arrangements** available to students who finance themselves?

4. Does the University offer **paid opportunities on campus** for doctoral students — research assistant, teaching assistant, or similar roles — that could be combined with full-time doctoral study?

5. Is there any support towards **accommodation costs**? The providers I have contacted require the year to be paid in three instalments, which is difficult to manage at once.

I would be grateful for any guidance you can give. I will be in Nicosia on **12 and 13 October 2026** and would be glad to discuss this in person if that is convenient.

Thank you very much for your time.

## Hinweise für Maria

- **Frage 4 ist die wertvollste.** Eine bezahlte Stelle als Research oder Teaching Assistant löst das Liquiditätsproblem dauerhaft, nicht nur einmalig — und sie ist an einer Universität deutlich häufiger als Härtefallfonds. Die Frage wurde in der früheren Anfrage an Herrn Georgiou gestellt, aber nur allgemein beantwortet; bei Herrn Kounoupis gehört sie in die richtige Zuständigkeit.
- **Die eigene Lage klar benennen, ohne zu klagen.** Eigenfinanzierung und Erstakademikerin sind bei bedarfsorientierter Förderung **bewertungsrelevante Tatsachen**, keine Entschuldigung. Der Text sagt das in zwei Sätzen und geht weiter.
- **Nicht dieselbe Frage doppelt stellen.** Zu Stipendien hat Herr Georgiou abschließend geantwortet; diese Mail fragt ausdrücklich nach etwas anderem und verweist höflich darauf.
